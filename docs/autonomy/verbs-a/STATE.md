# verbs-a — journal

The loop's journal for `docs/autonomy/verbs-a/QUEUE.md`: one entry per item,
newest at the bottom, in the shape `docs/autonomy/LOOP.md` prescribes.

## Opened 2026-08-30

The A9.1 real-model evaluation counted 136 verbs against 347 excluded routes,
47 of which excuse themselves with "a later intent set". This track turns its
share of those into verbs — or into exclusions that say honestly why an agent
must never do them.
